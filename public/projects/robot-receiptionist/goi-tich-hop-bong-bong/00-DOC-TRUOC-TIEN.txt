TOM TAT TRONG 30 GIAY
---------------------
De dua tro ly ao len Cong, quy don vi chi can DAN MOT DOAN MA khoang 16
dong vao mot Fragment HTML cua Liferay, giong het cach Cong da tung lam
voi tro ly ao truoc day.

  - Khong can lap trinh.
  - Khong can cai dat gi tren may chu cua Cong.
  - Khong can mo them cong (port), khong can cau hinh tuong lua.
  - Go bo bat cu luc nao: chi can xoa doan ma da dan.

Thoi gian du kien: 15-30 phut, bao gom ca kiem thu.

KHOA TEN MIEN - DOC KY MUC NAY
------------------------------
Trong doan ma co mot tham so "key". Day la khoa LAZINET cap rieng cho ten
mien hcc.quangtri.gov.vn. Nho khoa nay, nguoi dan hoi tren Cong KHONG bi
gioi han so cau hoi (trang tro ly ao cong khai co gioi han doi voi khach
chua dang nhap, de kiem soat chi phi).

  - Khoa nam trong ma nguon trang nen ai cung doc duoc. Dieu do la BINH
    THUONG va dung thiet ke, giong site key cua reCAPTCHA.
  - Khoa chi co hieu luc khi loi goi den tu dung ten mien da dang ky. Chep
    sang website khac se khong dung duoc.
  - Khi chay thu o may ca nhan (localhost) hoac moi truong thu nghiem co
    ten mien khac, tro ly van chay nhung se bao HET LUOT HOI sau mot so
    cau. Day la bieu hien DUNG, khong phai loi. Neu moi truong thu nghiem
    cua Cong co ten mien rieng, de nghi bao LAZINET de cap them khoa.
  - Tuyet doi khong sua tham so "key". Neu can cap lai khoa, LAZINET se
    gui khoa moi va Cong cap nhat lai doan ma.

Chi tiet o muc 5.1 cua tai lieu chinh.


MOT LUU Y QUAN TRONG
--------------------
Tren trang chu cua Cong hien VAN CON doan ma nhung tro ly ao cua don vi
cung cap truoc. Doan ma do van ve ra bong bong, nhung may chu phia don vi
do da ngung phuc vu nen nguoi dan bam vao chi nhan duoc thong bao loi.

De nghi go bo Fragment chua doan ma cu, de tranh hai bong bong cung nam o
mot goc man hinh va tranh de nguoi dan bam vao mot chuc nang khong con
hoat dong. Chi tiet o muc 4.4 cua tai lieu chinh.


HO TRO
------
LAZINET san sang huong dan truc tiep tu xa trong lan dan ma dau tien,
cung chay danh muc kiem thu va lap bien ban nghiem thu, cung nhu tra loi
cac cau hoi cua bo phan an toan thong tin.
